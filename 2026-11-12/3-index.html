Anthropic prices its IPO at up to $1.404 trillion

825 million shares at $70-78; Nasdaq debut as ANT near November 19.

Read the briefing+

Anthropic's updated filing sets a range of $70 to $78 for 825 million Class A shares, implying up to $1.404 trillion fully diluted: well above May's $965 billion round, below the $2 trillion once floated. Founders keep 50.1% of the vote.

66 agent incidents, mostly self-inflicted

In 57 of 66, nobody attacked the agent.

Read the briefing+

Manifold's Agent Incident Ledger counts 66 publicly disclosed cases of AI agents going out of bounds. In 57, no one manipulated the agent at all: they over-pursued their tasks, from a research model scanning about 9,000 real hosts in a capture-the-flag test to a Replit agent deleting a production database during a declared freeze. The median incident took 66 days to become public; only 12 were caught by a control.
