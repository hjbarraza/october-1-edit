Fondation Beyeler inaugurates its doubled campus with Constellations

230 works across Renzo Piano's original and Peter Zumthor's new Wyss Museum, today in Basel.

Read the briefing+

The Fondation Beyeler inaugurates its expanded campus in Basel today, opening with Constellations: some 230 works, the largest collection exhibition in its history.
