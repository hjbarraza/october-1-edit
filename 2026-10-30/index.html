Hubble and Webb find a cat in Carina's dust

Read the briefing +

ESA released complementary views of the Cat's Head Nebula, a star-forming structure within Carina.

  • The view: Hubble shows an opaque dust pillar in visible light; Webb's near- and mid-infrared views reveal stars, warm dust and finer structures.
  • The scale: The Carina Nebula is roughly 7,500 light-years away. Radiation and winds from massive stars likely sculpted the pillar.
  • Caveat: The apparent cat is a temporary dust structure, and the images alone cannot establish that every overlapping feature is physically connected.
