Vienna’s Stadttempel gets a careful second century

The two-hundred-year-old Stadttempel, the only Viennese synagogue to survive the 1938 pogroms, has reopened after a ten-month restoration.

Read the briefing +

The two-hundred-year-old Stadttempel, the only Viennese synagogue to survive the 1938 pogroms, has reopened after a ten-month restoration.

  • History: Josef Kornhäusel completed the oval synagogue in 1826; it was the only one in Vienna not destroyed in 1938.
  • Restoration: KENH restored the entrance and original stone, integrating acoustics, heating and cooling into curved seating and walls.
  • Why it matters: The update protects a rare survivor without freezing it into a museum piece.
