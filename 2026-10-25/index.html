Fresh images of the renovation of Rotterdam's great museum, closed since 2019.

Read the briefing +

Fresh images of the renovation of Rotterdam's great museum, closed since 2019.

  • What happened: Mecanoo released new images of the comprehensive renovation on October 23; the 25,000 m2 complex has been closed since 2019 over fire risk, water damage and asbestos.
  • Details: The plan unites three listed buildings, from 1935, 1972 and 1991, around a new entrance courtyard with a transparent Connector facade and a sunken Rotunda bringing daylight below ground.
  • Caveat: The Henket pavilion becomes a garden restaurant; a 2003 addition comes down. No reopening date is given.

Read the briefing +

New renderings for the Museum of Science and Industry's planned entrance hall, and the pushback against it.

  • What happened: Robert A.M. Stern Architects' one-story pavilion would restore the museum's original front-door approach in Jackson Park, replacing a multi-story underground entry.
  • Details: The 28-foot-tall structure spans 458 feet with grass-covered berms and two science gardens; the underground hall would become exhibition space.
