Google makes Gemini 4 Carbon public

Introductory price: $2 in, $10 out per million tokens.

Read the briefing+

Google has released Gemini 4 Carbon to the general public, two weeks after giving it to cybersecurity defenders. It calls Carbon its strongest model and positions it above Argon for the hardest multi-step coding and scientific research; Argon stays the pick for most everyday work. Launch pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output, about a quarter of Anthropic's Fable 5.2, and doubles at the end of November. A swarm feature on Antigravity, in preview, turns one prompt into a coordinated team of agents. Google's own benchmark picks put it behind Fable 5.2.

Released 2026-11-10

Kimi K3.1 Pro opens its weights, with a license catch

2.8 trillion parameters, 104 billion active, one million tokens of context.

Read the briefing+

Moonshot AI has published Kimi K3.1 Pro on Hugging Face: an open-weight, natively multimodal agentic model, a mixture-of-experts with 2.8 trillion total and 104 billion active parameters and a one-million-token context window, post-trained from Kimi K3 for coding, 3D creation and financial research. It ships under Moonshot's own license, not MIT or Apache: per the Hugging Face page, a licensee offering it as a service with revenue above $20 million over any 12 months needs a separate agreement.

OPEN SOURCE041 min read

Blender 5.3 learns Gaussian splats

Import, process in Geometry Nodes, render in EEVEE and Cycles.

Read the briefing+

Blender 5.3 adds native 3D Gaussian Splat support. Splats can be imported from PLY, SPZ and glTF files, stored as point clouds, processed with Geometry Nodes and rendered in EEVEE, Cycles and Workbench. The release also brings DLSS denoising, light dispersion, an EEVEE fisheye camera and ripple video editing. A reconstruction technique that lived in research demos now has a free, open-source home.

Read the briefing+

TECH071 min read

AI designs leaner quantum circuits

Harvard, QuEra and Anthropic: up to 39x on a combined metric.

Read the briefing+

A preprint from Harvard, QuEra and Anthropic, 'Discovering state-of-the-art quantum circuits with large language models', examines eight building blocks of larger quantum algorithms. In seven, AI-designed circuits cut both gate counts and qubits against baselines, by up to 39 times on a combined gate-and-qubit metric; in the eighth the search found a new trade-off. Useful quantum computing needs better hardware and cheaper algorithms. This pulls on the second curve.

A humanoid that learns from its operators

Embodied AI's wheeled AIR enters factory trials.

Read the briefing+

Swiss company Embodied AI unveiled AIR, a wheeled humanoid for repetitive, dexterous factory tasks, at Humanoid Europe in Munich. Board inquiry; senators push the AI Overwatch Act.

Fireworks: two October breaches, billing data taken

Secrets and AI datasets exposed; a cloud provider was one door.

Read the briefing+

AI infrastructure company Fireworks says a financially motivated group stole account and billing information and secrets in a breach it found on October 6. A second incident on October 23, in which a third-party cloud provider hosting its R&D machines was attacked, let intruders reach development machines and led to additional data impact, including invoices, payment history and AI-related datasets. Affected customers have been notified; Unit 42 is investigating. The AI supply chain is a target in its own right.

Nitori: up to 37.6 million records

Intruder likely inside since mid-October.

Read the briefing+

Japanese furniture retailer Nitori, often called the IKEA of Japan, confirmed on November 6 that a third party accessed a system and up to 37.62 million personal records may have leaked; the attacker likely had access since mid-October. Exposed fields may include membership numbers, names, emails, addresses, phone numbers, birth dates and gender, plus order data. Nitori says account passwords and credit card information were not exposed.

SPACE141 min read

New Glenn slips to January

Blue Origin also unveiled Northstar, a cheaper engine.

Read the briefing+

Blue Origin's return-to-flight mission for New Glenn has moved from December to January after the pad explosion in May, which the company traced to an oxygen valve on a BE-4 engine. The same day CEO Dave Limp unveiled Northstar, a simpler engine with half the parts and 30% less mass that Blue Origin says can be built at 25% of a BE-4's cost, with 715,000 pounds of starting thrust versus 640,000, and a hydrogen-oxygen third stage, GS3. The aim is a rocket that can fly more than 1,000 times a year. First, one flight.

BepiColombo arrives at Mercury on November 21

The second orbiter ever, and the first with two probes.

Read the briefing+

ESA and JAXA's BepiColombo is scheduled to enter orbit around Mercury on November 21, making it the second mission to orbit the planet after NASA's MESSENGER and the first two-probe system. The two orbiters separate on December 10. Launched in 2018, it has shed speed through nine planetary flybys; thruster trouble pushed arrival back about a year.

Scheduled 2026-11-21
