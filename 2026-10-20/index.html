New York's mayor used an AI Now Institute event to argue that cities should regulate AI when Washington will not.

Microsoft wants a security layer between firms and their agents

New Guardian Agents and Defender controls treat enterprise AI agents like potential insider threats.

Read the briefing +

Microsoft's new agent-security pitch is blunt: do not let the model supplier police the model.

Nick Cammarata's 26,000-word interpretability post says models may be organized enough to map, if researchers look harder.

Read the briefing +

A viral interpretability claim has given AI safety researchers a rare week of optimism.

  • Claim: Cammarata says he mapped whole circuits after revisiting the idea that unrelated concepts are hopelessly tangled inside models.
  • Example: He argues a neuron once described as responding to both cats and cars may really be responding to a shared V-shaped visual pattern.

Japan launches a sample-return mission to a Martian moon

MMX is on its way to Phobos with a rover, two sampling systems and a return capsule aimed at Earth in 2031.

Read the briefing +

Japan's H3 rocket has started the first mission intended to bring material back from the Mars system.

  • Mission: The $350 million MMX spacecraft will study Phobos and Deimos to learn whether they are captured asteroids or debris from a giant impact.
  • Hardware: A 55-pound Franco-German rover will land first; the main craft later touches down to collect at least 10 grams using JAXA's corer and Blue Origin's pneumatic sampler.
