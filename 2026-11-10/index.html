Anthropic splits its frontier model in two

Fable 5.2 ships to everyone; Mythos 5.2 serves vetted researchers.

Read the briefing+

Anthropic has released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, the same underlying frontier model split by safeguards. Fable carries cyber and biology safeguards and is available to all users; Mythos is open to organizations in Anthropic's verification programs. Outputs run up to 30% faster at a lower price, $8 per million input tokens. In prerelease testing the models designed three working electronic devices from scratch, solved two open problems in the physics of black holes and formalized the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours. The frontier is becoming a licensed product.

Friday the 13th: catch the whole rocket

Starship Flight 15 attempts the first double tower catch.

Read the briefing+

SpaceX is targeting Friday, November 13, for Starship Flight 15: the first attempt to return both the Super Heavy booster and the Ship upper stage to the launch tower for catch, which would mark the first full recovery of an orbital launch system. The profile runs more than 22 hours and about 14 orbits, deploying 26 next-generation Starlink V3 satellites before Ship comes home. SpaceX has caught the booster three times; it has never tried with the ship.

SPACE151 min read

A new road to Mars, taken twice

ESCAPADE completes the first powered Earth flybys.

Read the briefing+

NASA's twin ESCAPADE spacecraft have each completed a powered Earth flyby, gravity assist plus a nine-minute engine burn, and are now en route to Mars for arrival in September 2027. After launching when the planets were misaligned, the pair loitered near Lagrange point 2 for a year, then slingshotted home. The payoff: a route that allows launching to Mars almost anytime, not once every two years.
